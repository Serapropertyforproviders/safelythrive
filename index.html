The Framework

Decides what happens

The Safeguarding Learning & Accountability Framework: clear thresholds, four levels of response, named responsibilities and deadlines, and eleven ready-to-use tools.

I-CAASE

Manages the hardest cases

Independent Child-on-child Abuse Assessment, Safeguarding & Early Intervention. Separate assessments and plans for both children, with the harmed child's safety first.

My Three™

Works with each child

Structured activities that help children name who they are, where they are going and what keeps them safe, alongside a trusted adult.

Prevention

Whole-class My Three™ sessions, every child names a trusted adult, and staff are trained.

Early help

A recorded decision within 1 school day and a short plan agreed with the family.

Coordination

Multi-agency planning. Serious or persistent harm between children uses I-CAASE.

Protection

An immediate report to child protective services or law enforcement, as the law requires.

Mandated reporting always comes first. Nothing we teach delays or replaces a report that state law requires.

My Three™: the work with each child

My Three™ is Seraphina Ely's own original programme. Children travel through three lands, and in each one they build words for who they are, where they are going and what keeps them safe and well, alongside a trusted adult.

Land of Me

Who I am, what I need, my strengths and my talents.

Land of Dreams

My goals and the steps that will get me there.

Land of Joy

What keeps me calm and happy, and the safe adults I can tell.

Elementary (primary)Picture-led worksheets, whole-class sessions and naming a trusted adult.
Middle and high schoolEmpathy, friendships, online life and planning ahead, in small groups or one to one.
Young adultCollege and university students and care leavers: independence, wellbeing and goals.

My Three™ runs through everything we offer. It is Level 1 of the Framework, and in I-CAASE cases each child works through My Three™ separately with their own trusted adult.

How safe is your setting?

Ten honest questions for schools, youth organisations and churches. Your answers stay on your device. Requirements vary by state, so this is a starting point, not an audit or legal advice.

Have you named a designated safeguarding lead and a trained deputy, in writing?

Have all staff and volunteers completed child-abuse and mandated-reporter training that meets your state's requirements in the last 12 months?

Does a written procedure tell every adult who to tell, when, and how to report to child protective services or law enforcement?

Are background checks and references completed before anyone starts working with young people?

Do you have written rules on one-to-one contact, transport, trips and communication, including online and by phone, between adults and young people?

Do your board and senior leaders receive regular safeguarding training and review safeguarding reports every quarter?

Do you have a plan for harm between children that supports both children separately?

When a child is suspended or absent, does someone contact them the same day?

Can every child or young person name a trusted adult in your setting?

Do staff and volunteers receive support, such as supervision or debriefing, when they carry difficult concerns?

Questions people ask

Does your training replace our state’s mandated reporter training?

No. Our training supports it. Each state sets its own mandated reporter rules, so we help you understand and follow them alongside the training your state requires.

Is everything online?

Yes, for now. All training, reviews and consultations are delivered live online. In-person work in Southern California is planned as the practice grows.

What are I-CAASE and My Three™?

I-CAASE is a structured process for serious or persistent harm between children: each child is assessed separately and gets their own plan. My Three™ is the structured work with each child, used at every level from whole-class sessions to one-to-one support.

Is this right for a small school, organisation or church?

Yes. Our founding rates are set so smaller organisations can afford them, and the Safe Church Pathway is designed for churches with fewer than 300 people on a Sunday.

Do people get a certificate?

Yes. Everyone who attends receives a Safely Thrive certificate of attendance, which you can keep on file for your insurer and board.

Will you work directly with children?

No. We train and support the adults who work with children, and they deliver My Three™ sessions themselves.

Does this make us compliant with our state’s laws?

No. We help you strengthen practice and prepare for scrutiny from insurers and boards. Requirements vary by state, so we work alongside your counsel and approved providers.

Why a UK-informed approach?

England's schools have a named Designated Safeguarding Lead, clear thresholds and strong governance oversight. We bring those practices to US settings, adapted to state law and procedures.
